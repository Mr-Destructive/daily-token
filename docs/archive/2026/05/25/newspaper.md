### The Switchberry and the Illusion of Pure Hardware
Source: https://www.hackster.io/news/the-switchberry-turns-a-raspberry-pi-compute-module-4-into-a-time-focused-ethernet-switch-85ecd2eb97b2
HN: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48264099
By rigging a Compute Module 4 into an Ethernet switch, the Switchberry project attempts to reclaim precise time synchronization from bloated modern networking stacks. It exposes a bitter trade-off: achieving deterministic latency now requires building your own bespoke hardware because commercial silicon has largely abandoned the ethos of simple, predictable craft.

### The Middleware Creep: Aspire Attempts to Automate the Plumbing
Source: https://aspire.dev/
HN: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48264049
As standard application stacks grow increasingly fragmented, Aspire steps in to coordinate the mess of front ends, containers, and databases under a single orchestrator. It promises convenience, but replacing legible configuration with another layer of automated abstraction usually means trade-offs in long-term debuggability.
