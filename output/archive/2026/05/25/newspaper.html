SOURCE: HACKERNEWS | HN DISCUSSION

Usborne’s decision to release its vintage coding books for free reminds us of a time when programming required understanding the machine, rather than pasting generated code blocks. The Switchberry and the Illusion of Pure Hardware

SOURCE: HACKERNEWS | HN DISCUSSION

By rigging a Compute Module 4 into an Ethernet switch, the Switchberry project attempts to reclaim precise time synchronization from bloated modern networking stacks. It exposes a bitter trade-off: achieving deterministic latency now requires building your own bespoke hardware because commercial silicon has largely abandoned the ethos of simple, predictable craft.
