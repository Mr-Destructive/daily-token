Source: https://kotaku.com/playstation-3-emulator-devs-politely-ask-that-people-stop-flooding-it-with-ai-code-pull-requests-2000694656
HN: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48089263
The surge in AI-generated pull requests is not a failure of technology, but a failure of individual accountability. The core issue is the disconnect between the low barrier to generating code and the high level of expertise required to document, test, and justify those changes within complex, undocumented environments. Quote: The problem is really behavioural, not the tooling.
