HN: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48072319
The community views AI not as a replacement for logic, but as an ergonomic evolution similar to the transition from hand tools to power tools. ### The Iron Constraint of Japanese Jurisprudence
Source: https://sundaicity.com/blogs/getting-arrested-in-japan
HN: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48078647
When navigating the Japanese legal system, travelers must understand that the lack of procedural transparency is compounded by a rigid social order where bribery is non-functional and can actively worsen a detainee's position. The system prioritizes psychological pressure and strict compliance over the physical comforts or 'special treatment' found in other jurisdictions. Quote: Japan is a society where it's difficult to get special treatment by giving money, not just to officials. If you try to use bribes, you'll only be looked down upon and put in a worse position.
