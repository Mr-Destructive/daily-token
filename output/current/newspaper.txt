TOP INSIGHTS & ADVICE
------------------------------------------------
* The Utility vs. Craftsmanship Paradox
  https://tonsky.me/blog/fall-of-native/
  The shift from native to Electron apps represents a prioritization of universal accessibility and brand consistency over hardware efficiency. While purists lament the loss of low-level mastery, the industry has largely accepted 'deep stacks of abstraction' to ensure software works everywhere immediately, even at the cost of performance. Quote: The medium doesn't really matter as much as the result... and most software is about achieving a result.

* The Verification Bottleneck: From Vibe-Coding to Formal Specs
  https://leodemoura.github.io/blog/2026/02/28/when-ai-writes-the-worlds-software.html
  AI generation doesn't create new quality problems; it exposes existing ones by accelerating the code-to-verification loop. The community consensus is that we are shifting from a 'write-and-check' workflow to one where the primary human value lies in defining rigorous, formal specifications and high-stakes QA, as AI often optimizes for passing tests rather than true logical correctness. Quote: The LLM happily churns out unit tests which are simply reinforcing the existing behaviour of the code.

* From Dismissal to Coercion: The Expert's Edge in LLM Reasoning
  https://www-cs-faculty.stanford.edu/~knuth/papers/claude-cycles.pdf
  The community is shifting from dismissing LLMs to recognizing them as powerful probability distributions that automate the 'search space' of logic. While models still hit 'dumb zones' or require manual generalization to reach formal proofs, the core value lies in an expert's ability to use 'summoning spells' to coerce the model into productive patterns that were previously limited by the speed of human cognition. Quote: Experts will naturally use these systems more productively, because they know how to coerce models into these patterns.
