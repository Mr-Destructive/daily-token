  https://modolap.com/publication/hn-analysis-1
  The community highlights the power of combining AI (like Codex/Claude) with structured data tools (SQLite, DuckDB, Parquet) to unlock rapid, ad-hoc analysis of massive datasets—without heavy infrastructure. Key applications include tracking platform health (e.g., toxicity, sentiment shifts post-ChatGPT) and uncovering historical tech trends (e.g., MongoDB’s early dominance over PostgreSQL). The workflow emphasizes *automated schema design* (AI-generated SQLite models) and *iterative exploration* (AI-assisted queries/graphs), reducing friction between raw data and actionable insights. A cautionary note emerges: AI’s role in mining public discourse may alter participation dynamics due to privacy concerns or reduced need for human Q&A. Quote: "inspect and understand the downloaded data in directory /path/..., then come up with an sqlite data model for doing detailed analytics and ingest everything into an sqlite db in data.sqlite, and document the model in model.md."
